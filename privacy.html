Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Offline-First & Ad-Free by Default

OffGrid Music is designed to operate 100% locally on your Android device with no advertisements, no ad SDKs, and no required account. Your audio files remain strictly on your device.

2. Optional Cloud Backup & Sync

If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In or Email & Password, OffGrid Music backs up only your library metadata (favorites, playlists, smart playlists, listening statistics, lyric sync offsets, folder rules, and preferences) to your encrypted cloud snapshot. Your actual music files are never uploaded to cloud storage.

3. Optional Gemini AI Lyric Studio

AI translation and transcription features are disabled by default. If you provide your own Gemini API key, it is stored encrypted locally on your device and is never included in cloud backups.

4. Contact & Open-Source Transparency

Inspect the Android app source code anytime on the official OffGrid Music GitHub repository, or reach out at offgridmusicsupport@gmail.com.
