Chapter 03 · Audio Engine & Sonic Studio

Studio Sound. Zero Compromise.

From 0–12 second equal-power crossfade and 5-band graphic EQ to A–B Loops, Timestamp Pins, and 7 seekbar waveforms.

Audio & Sound Engine

  • Gapless Playback & 0–12s Equal-Power Crossfade
  • Custom Play Fade-In & Pause Fade-Out (0–5,000 ms)
  • 5-Band Graphic EQ + 11 Presets, Bass Boost & 3D Surround
  • Normalize Audio, Stereo Balance & Notification Ducking
  • Live Output Switcher (Speaker, Aux, Bluetooth, USB DAC)

Sonic Studio & Smart Controls

  • Repeat This Song (1–10×) compatible with Crossfade & Sleep Timer
  • A–B Loop: Repeat any chorus, solo, or practice section
  • Timestamp Bookmarks: Pin Intro, Beat Drop, Chorus, or Solo
  • Sonic DNA Radio: Instant 25-track vibe-matched mix
  • Album Art Swipe Gestures, Shake to Skip & On-Device ID3 Tag Editor

7 Waveform & 4 Mini-Player Styles

  • Choose visually from Sleek Line, Snake Sine, Symmetric, Dotted, Squiggly, Wave, and Bar
  • 4 Mini-Player styles: Floating Pill, Bottom Bar, Artwork Glass & Compact Slim
  • Dual-Mode Sleep Timer: Time-based (up to 180 min) or Track-based (up to 50 tracks)
  • 90/120Hz High Refresh Rate smoothness across phones & tablets
