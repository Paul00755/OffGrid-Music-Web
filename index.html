Help, FAQ & Feedback

Frequently Asked Questions

App safety, Google Sign-In, cloud backups, Off-Grid Jam, Gemini AI API key help, playback tips & more.

14 questions
Trust & Safety The app is not published on the Google Play Store. Is it really safe to use?
Yes. OffGrid Music is completely open source on GitHub with zero ads, zero trackers, and zero hidden permissions. Every APK is built and published transparently through GitHub Releases so anyone can inspect the source code and verify every build.
Account & Backup In what ways can I sign in to OffGrid Music?
You can sign in with Google Sign-In or create an Email & Password account—or skip sign-in completely and use OffGrid Music 100% locally with no account required.
Trust & Safety If I use Google Sign-In, will my data get stolen?
No. Google Sign-In is used solely to authenticate your optional cloud backup account. OffGrid Music never accesses your Gmail, Drive, or contacts, never sells data, and never uploads your local audio files.
Account & Backup What data is backed up when I sign in?
Your Favorites, Custom Playlists, Smart Playlists, Listening Statistics & History, Synced Lyrics & Per-Song Timing Offsets, Folder Include/Exclude Rules, Custom Home Feed Configurations, and App Preferences (theme & audio EQ). Your actual MP3/FLAC music files and any personal Gemini API key are never uploaded.
Account & Backup Where is my backed-up data stored?
Backups are stored as an encrypted cloud snapshot tied strictly to your authenticated Firebase account. You can trigger Backup Now, Sync / Restore Now, or schedule automatic Daily, Weekly, or Monthly backups—or export local JSON backups anytime.
Off-Grid Jam How does Off-Grid Jam work?
OffGrid Jam lets 2 to 10 friends listen in real-time sync. Host a room and share the Invite Code, Invite Link, or QR Code. Participants stay synced to playback, and approved local songs (up to 25 MB) can be shared directly device-to-device without uploading your library to cloud storage.
AI & Lyrics Is my Gemini API key safe?
Yes. AI Lyrics Studio is turned off by default. If you choose to connect your own Gemini API key, it is stored encrypted strictly on your device and is excluded from all cloud backups and diagnostics exports.
AI & Lyrics How do I get and add a Gemini API key for lyrics translation or transcription?
Create a free API key in Google AI Studio, open OffGrid Music Settings → Lyrics & AI Studio, enable Optional Gemini AI Lyrics Studio, and paste your key. Once active, you can translate synced lyrics into English, Tamil/Tanglish, Hindi, or Malayalam—or transcribe audio into timestamped .lrc lyrics with one-tap Undo.
AI & Lyrics I'm facing API key errors — what should I do?
Double-check that your key has no extra spaces, hasn't hit its free-tier rate limit in Google AI Studio, and that your device has an active internet connection when running AI translation/transcription. If the issue persists, generate a fresh key or email us a screenshot at offgridmusicsupport@gmail.com.
Playback & Library What is Smart Shuffle (Smart Queue) and how does it work?
Smart Shuffle and Sonic DNA Radio analyze your library and currently playing track's vibe to build fresh, balanced queues without repeating the same handful of songs back-to-back—while keeping full 1:1 drag-and-drop control over your Up Next queue.
Playback & Library Does OffGrid Music work completely offline?
Yes! Playing local music (across 12 formats including MP3, FLAC, OPUS, WAV, M4A, and AIFF), synced .lrc lyrics, 5-Band EQ, Wrapped Stats, Playlists, and Home Screen Widgets all work 100% offline—perfect for flights, commutes, and off-grid listening.
Playback & Library Why are some of my local audio files not appearing in my library?
Check two settings in Library & Scanner: (1) Short Audio Clip Filter (set between 0–180s to hide ringtones/voice notes—lower it if short tracks are filtered out), and (2) Folder Include / Exclude Rules to ensure the folder containing your songs is included, then tap refresh or run a scan.
Playback & Library How can I customize the Home Feed, Bottom Bar, or Now Playing screen?
Open Settings → Screen Layout Studio to preview and switch between 4 Now Playing, 4 Home, 4 Playlists, and 3 Lyrics UI layouts (including Combo View). In Appearance & Home Feed settings, you can also show/hide/reorder Home sections, rearrange the 5 bottom bar tabs, and pick from 7 waveform styles and 4 mini-player styles.
Updates & Tips Will installing a new app update delete my playlists, favorites, or settings?
No. Install any newer OffGrid Music APK directly over your existing app—do not uninstall first. All of your playlists, favorites, synced lyrics offsets, custom cover art, listening stats, and settings stay right where you left them.
